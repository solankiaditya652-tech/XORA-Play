FAQ
Common questions
How do I play online?
Tap Create Game to get your Join Code, share it with a friend. Your friend opens Join Game and enters your code.
Why does online need internet?
Online play connects two devices directly (peer-to-peer), so both phones need an internet connection.
What is my Join Code?
Your Join Code is your personal ID (see Profile). Friends use it to connect to your game.
Does it work offline?
Yes — Single Player and Pass & Play work with no internet. Only online mode needs it.
How do I install the app?
Open Settings → Install App, or use your browser menu → “Add to Home Screen”.
